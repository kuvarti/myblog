## Rule
Talk to the owner in Turkish. Do not translate or localize technical terms — keep every technical concept in its original English form (e.g. commit, endpoint, middleware, cache, service, build, deploy, token). Write the Turkish sentence around the English technical term; never invent a Turkish equivalent for it.

## Rationale
The owner prefers Turkish conversation but finds Turkified technical vocabulary harder to follow than the plain English terms.

## Applies to
Chat/conversation only. Written repository artifacts are still English — see [[documentation-language]].
